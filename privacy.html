Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Citadel Apex Solutions ("we", "us") is a technology company based at Plot 6C Abdulrahman Okene Close, Off Ligali Ayorinde Street, Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria. This policy explains what personal information we handle through this website and our services, and the choices you have. We aim to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Information we collect

  • Enquiries. If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or the contact form, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number or email and your message.
  • Website use. This website does not use advertising trackers. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (for example IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability.
  • Service data. When we build or operate a product for a client, we process the data that product needs, under the client's instructions and our agreement with them.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide quotes and services.
  • To operate, secure and improve our website and services.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

AI assistants and call or message handling

Some of our products, including our AI receptionist, answer calls or messages on behalf of a business. These assistants identify themselves as automated, use only information approved by the business, and may use trusted service providers (such as telephony, speech and messaging providers) to deliver the service. Retention periods are agreed with each business and kept as short as practical.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run our services (for example hosting, messaging and communications providers), when the law requires it, or with your consent. Providers act on our instructions.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires, and we use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. Contact us using the details below. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
