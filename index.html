Our product

Meet Sarah, the AI receptionist.

Sarah is our own AI receptionist, designed for Nigerian small businesses. She answers telephone calls and WhatsApp messages, shares only the business information the owner has approved, takes messages and passes questions to staff, and says plainly that she is an automated assistant.

  • Answers calls in natural Nigerian English
  • Uses only approved business information, never guesses
  • Takes messages and hands over to your staff
  • Discloses that she is an AI assistant

Sarah is currently in pilot. Interested in early access? Talk to us.
